ECHO
SHOT

A one-thumb shooter where every missed second comes back. Fire once, rewind, and your past shots fire with you.

Core loop

  1. AimHold to slow time and line up a shot. Bullets pierce and bounce twice.
  2. RewindThe loop ends and the room resets. Your shot is now an echo on the timeline.
  3. StackFire again alongside your echoes until every target falls in the same loop.

Why it could work

  • It keeps the feel of an action shooter (slow-mo aim, one decisive shot) in a 20 to 40 second level.
  • The rewind turns a reflex game into a planning puzzle, so it suits players who like puzzle and casual games too.
  • Stars reward fewer loops, which gives skilled players a reason to replay without blocking anyone.
  • Levels are cheap to author: a level is a short list of walls, targets and paths.

What this build has

8handmade levels
4mechanics: pierce, bounce, movers, civilians
100%levels checked solvable by a solver script

Next tests

  • Hybrid-casual layer: upgrades between levels (a third bounce, a split shot, a slower echo) and echo skins.
  • Boss rooms where the boss moves differently each loop, so echoes must be re-planned.
  • A daily timeline puzzle with a shared par for every player.
  • Measure level 1 to 3 completion, where players fail, and how often they retry for stars.

How it was built

Prototype by Mohamed Mady, built with Claude (AI) in one session: concept, then a playable build, then level tuning. A small solver script fires thousands of test shots per level to confirm each one can be beaten and to set the star targets.
